# Salla7ly (صلحلي)

## 1. Project Overview

Salla7ly is a mobile application designed to connect customers with reliable home maintenance service providers. It aims to simplify the process of finding, comparing, and booking technicians while improving transparency, trust, and service quality.

The application addresses common challenges faced by customers, such as difficulty finding trusted technicians, unclear pricing, uncertainty about service quality, and the inconvenience of arranging maintenance through phone calls and personal recommendations.

Salla7ly provides an organized platform where users can explore services, compare technician offers, schedule appointments, and track their maintenance requests.

## 2. Project Idea

The idea behind Salla7ly is to create a convenient and reliable platform that brings customers and home maintenance technicians together in one place.

Instead of searching randomly for technicians or relying only on personal recommendations, users can browse available service providers, review their ratings and experience, compare prices and offers, and choose the most suitable option for their needs.

The platform also aims to improve the technician experience by helping service providers reach more customers, manage service requests, and organize their appointments.

## 3. Project Objectives

- Make it easier for customers to find reliable home maintenance technicians.
- Provide clear information about technicians, their experience, ratings, and service offers.
- Allow customers to compare available offers before making a decision.
- Simplify the service request and appointment scheduling process.
- Improve communication and transparency between customers and technicians.
- Enable customers to track their service requests and technician arrival.
- Help technicians manage their requests and reach potential customers.
- Improve the overall home maintenance experience.

## 4. Project Scope & Features

### Customer Features

- User registration and login.
- Browse home maintenance service categories.
- Search for suitable service providers.
- View technician profiles, experience, and ratings.
- Submit service requests with issue descriptions and photos.
- Select preferred appointment dates and times.
- Receive and compare technician offers.
- Track service request status and technician arrival.
- Rate and review completed services.
- View service details and related reports.

### Technician Features

- Create and manage a technician profile.
- Receive and manage service requests.
- Submit service offers, including prices and estimated completion time.
- Manage appointments and assigned tasks.
- Track service request progress.
- Build a professional reputation through customer ratings and reviews.

### Admin Features

- Manage customer and technician accounts.
- Monitor service requests and their statuses.
- Manage service categories.
- Monitor technician performance.
- Review ratings, complaints, and customer feedback.
- Access information that supports platform management and performance evaluation.

### Main Service Categories

- Plumbing.
- Electrical maintenance.
- Air conditioning maintenance.
- Carpentry.
- Other essential home maintenance services.

## 8. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

The following Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) are proposed to measure the effectiveness, reliability, and overall performance of the Salla7ly platform.

These indicators focus on the application's main objectives, including building trust, simplifying the booking process, ensuring service quality, and improving the experience of customers and service providers.

| KPI | Description | Measurement Method |
|---|---|---|
| Technician Verification Rate | Measures the percentage of registered technicians who successfully complete the verification process. | Verified Technicians / Total Registered Technicians × 100 |
| Booking Conversion Rate | Measures the percentage of service requests that result in confirmed bookings. | Confirmed Bookings / Total Service Requests × 100 |
| Quote Comparison Rate | Measures how often customers compare available technician offers before selecting a provider. | Requests with Compared Quotes / Requests with Available Quotes × 100 |
| On-Time Arrival Rate | Evaluates technicians' commitment to their scheduled arrival times. | On-Time Arrivals / Total Completed Visits × 100 |
| ETA Accuracy | Measures the accuracy of the estimated arrival time compared to the technician's actual arrival time. | Average Difference Between Estimated and Actual Arrival Times |
| Service Completion Rate | Measures the percentage of confirmed service bookings successfully completed. | Completed Services / Total Confirmed Bookings × 100 |
| Payment Success Rate | Measures the percentage of successful payment transactions through supported payment methods. | Successful Payments / Total Payment Attempts × 100 |
| Customer Satisfaction Rate | Evaluates customer satisfaction with service quality and the overall application experience. | Average Customer Rating |

These KPIs will help assess the platform's performance, identify operational challenges, and measure improvements in customer experience and service provider reliability.

Note: These are proposed performance indicators. Actual performance results and target values will be determined after implementation, testing, and data collection. The Payment Success Rate KPI depends on the payment methods supported in the final version.
