## 📊 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

The following Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) are proposed to measure the effectiveness, reliability, and overall performance of the Salla7ly platform.

These indicators focus on the main objectives of the application, including building trust, simplifying the booking process, ensuring service quality, and improving the experience of customers and service providers.

| KPI | Description | Measurement Method |
|---|---|---|
| Technician Verification Rate | Measures the percentage of registered technicians who successfully complete the verification process. | Verified Technicians / Total Registered Technicians × 100 |
| Booking Conversion Rate | Measures the percentage of service requests that result in confirmed bookings. | Confirmed Bookings / Total Service Requests × 100 |
| Quote Comparison Rate | Measures how often customers compare available technician offers before selecting a provider. | Requests with Compared Quotes / Requests with Available Quotes × 100 |
| On-Time Arrival Rate | Evaluates technicians' commitment to their scheduled arrival times. | On-Time Arrivals / Total Completed Visits × 100 |
| ETA Accuracy | Measures the accuracy of the estimated arrival time compared to the technician's actual arrival time. | Average Difference Between Estimated and Actual Arrival Times |
| Service Completion Rate | Measures the percentage of confirmed service bookings successfully completed. | Completed Services / Total Confirmed Bookings × 100 |
| Payment Success Rate | Measures the percentage of successful payment transactions through supported payment methods. | Successful Payments / Total Payment Attempts × 100 |
| Customer Satisfaction Rate | Evaluates customer satisfaction with service quality and the overall application experience. | Average Customer Rating |

These KPIs will help assess the platform's performance, identify operational challenges, and measure improvements in customer experience and service provider reliability.

*Note: These are proposed performance indicators. Actual performance results and target values will be determined after implementation, testing, and data collection. Payment-related KPIs depend on the final supported payment methods.*
